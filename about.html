Our Founder

Meet Chel-C Lewis

Luna's Esthetics was founded by Chel-C Lewis — a passionate, certified esthetician whose love for beauty and skin science drove her to create one of Trinidad's most sought-after spa and medspa experiences.

What began as a dedication to helping clients feel confident in their own skin has grown into a full luxury spa and medspa offering everything from advanced facials and laser treatments to body contouring, intimate brightening, and weight loss solutions.

Chel-C believes that beauty is not a one-size-fits-all concept — every client deserves a treatment plan as unique as they are. That philosophy is woven into every service at Luna's.
